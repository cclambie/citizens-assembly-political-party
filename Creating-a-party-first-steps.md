# Creating a party - first steps
## Timeline 
I see the only way to create a party of differenc, that would include the community independents would be to start with the community in any jurisdiction.

To do this you need to start with process of bringing the community together to discuss what matters to them.

So here is how I would do that.

1. 6-8 months -  Community Conversations
2. 3 months   - Citizens Assemblies at a local / state level - where the randomly selected citizens review all the comments and then create recommendations
3. 3 months   - Citizens Assembly to bring the recommendations from the previous level into a policy document
4. Naturally the process of the above would create community leaders that each community could get behind - so selection is from the facilitators of the community conversations.
Leaders should not self select - but be selected by the community, and encouraged by them - as the best leaders do not "want" to be the leader - that is why they are the best pick.

For 2028 this could look like this
1. Sep 2026 - Mar 2027 - Conversations had
2. Apr 2027 - Jun 2027 - State assemblies
3. Jul 2027 - Sep 2027 - National assembly - Mandate of Manifest
4. Aug 2027 - Oct 2027 - Candidate selection (Election likely called anytime in this period)
5. 	 2027   - May 2028 - Election

## Kitchen Table/ Lounge Room conversations
By encouraging and facilitating conversations to happen around the place, then you are growing the group of people that have civic duty. You are encouraging people to have difficult discussions and also to meet other people in their community and empathise with their position - be it rich vs poor in a particular community or 1st generation immigrant vs 8th generation immigrant (yes we are all immigrants except for first nations).

## Citizens Assemblies
The local level citizens assembly would be volunteer run and probably self selecting. That is due to the nature of the smaller population of an area, and the cost of running these at the professional level we will run the national one is prohibitive until real power and budget can be allocated to them. The problems and issues will be real, and recording them will be important.
The filtering of the issues will be done by a really random selection of people, so this step is super important - but is not a final step to hearing the real "silent majority" which the sortition process in the next step will address.

## Party Manifest - Sortition Citizens Assembly
Running a true citizens assembly across 3-4 months with a random selection of voters across the whole country with people from all walks of life invited to participate would make for a real mandate. Once this group is selected to cover geography, income, gender and education demographics - with approximately 50 people in the group.

- Assembly meet 3-6 times. 
- At least 3 times IRL.
- Experts are invited to submit written, audio and video submissions that are accesible to all
- Video conferences held to hear expert advice selected by the assembly
- The assembly takes all the evidence onboard and creates the core policy of the party
- This policy becomes the manifest that the party takes to the election

### Experts
"Ask 3 experts in a field their opinion, you get 3 different answers"

Experts, consultants and think tanks are welcome to submit reports to contribute their thoughts. The final recommendations will be selected by the assembly. 
- The assembly asks for expert advice, that they choose
- The assembly can ask for experts to debate one another, or for more indepth answers to certain parts
- The assembly deliberates over the expert advice
- The assembly selects which advice they believe to be true
